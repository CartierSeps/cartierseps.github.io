Requirements to be featured as an org repo

Meet all four criteria before submitting your application.

01

Include an MIT License

It should be common practice already, but have a file titled LICENSE under your repository and fill out an MIT LICENSE. For more info, visit the license-guide repo on the Vectorial Collective page. Understand what this license is and what it does.

02

Write a One-Page Project Report

Write a one page minimum report on your project, what it is, what it does, and why it is relevant and important. It doesn't have to be anything crazy, it is a mini-application sent to admin to get your project approved. If you do want the report to be added to your project, just make a text file on your repo and copy and paste it there.

03

Your Project Must Be Complete

Have your project completely done. This means all the files are working and tested and it has a readme.md and LICENSE file.

Include a line that says: "Author: @your_username" — you can copy and paste this directly into the last line of your README.md file.

$echo "Author: @your_username" >> README.md
04

Fill Out the Discord Application

Visit #application on our Discord and fill out an application completely and honestly. This is how we maintain validity and integrity to preserve credit and recognition.

Ready to apply? The form takes less than 10 minutes.
