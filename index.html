The Forge Workflow

How Contributions Work

We provide an inclusive, high-standard peer-review track designed for high school & university students looking to master collaborative engineering.

01

Claim an Issue (C++ or Python)

Pick up a curated good-first-issue on GitHub. Whether you want to optimize cache locality and vectorization in C++ or implement physics algorithms in Python, we have tasks tailored for both tracks.

02

Live Pair-Programming

Join our Discord channel #the-forge for CMake/compiler setups (clang++/g++), Python venvs, numerical precision checks, and automated GitHub CI feedback.

03

Merge & Permanent Credit

Get your pull request merged with permanent co-author credit in tagged releases, commit manifests, and project documentation.
